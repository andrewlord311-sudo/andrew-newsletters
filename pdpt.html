🌱 A new counselling & hypnotherapy practice, with rooms to rent, in Feering

Rise Therapy Centre, on London Road in Feering (CO5 9SD) — a few miles up the A12 from Colchester, on the Kelvedon side — runs its own counselling and hypnotherapy practice alongside two rooms available to rent to other practitioners, with a shared kitchenette, free parking, and rates from around £12 for an hour and a quarter via Fresha. Its own practitioner, Sophie Milburn, is a qualified counsellor and hypnotherapist with more than five years' experience working with anxiety, stress and burnout.

Worth knowing Feering isn't Colchester proper, but it's close enough — a short drive up the A12 — to matter to anyone in the area weighing up room-hire options, and it joins the growing list of small independent spaces (alongside The Lodge in Ardleigh and Flow in West Mersea) that sit outside the bigger multi-room centres like UK Therapy Rooms. Worth watching whether it grows into a multi-practitioner setup the way The Turner Centre and Stockwell have.

🔬 Research worth knowing about

One genuinely new pooled analysis this week, on a condition this newsletter hasn't covered in depth before: social anxiety.

New this week: does short-term psychodynamic psychotherapy help with social anxiety? A systematic review and meta-analysis in BMC Psychology pooled eleven randomised controlled trials — 1,167 patients in total — testing short-term psychodynamic psychotherapy (STPP) for social anxiety disorder. Against waiting-list controls, STPP produced a large, statistically reliable improvement in social anxiety symptoms (Hedges' g = −0.97), with a similar benefit on secondary measures of depressive symptoms. In plain terms: people who got STPP did clearly and substantially better than people who got nothing, which is a real result — but because the comparison group was a waitlist rather than an established rival treatment like CBT, the study can't say whether psychodynamic therapy is better, worse, or about the same as other options already used for social anxiety. It shows the treatment works; it doesn't yet show how it ranks.
