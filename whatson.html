Week of 10 August 2026

This week's headline is Colchester Pride's return to Firstsite on Saturday, with a high-street parade at 10:40am followed by a full day of live music, a market and family-friendly activity on the Main Stage. The same weekend, the Smoke & Fire Festival brings championship barbecue and live-fire cooking to Maldon's Promenade Park, while Colchester Castle's Museum of the Moon and Firstsite's Holiday Fun Canteen continue through the school holidays.

⭐ Pick of the week

Colchester Pride 2026 — a day of indoor and outdoor Pride celebration at Firstsite: a high-street parade, a Main Stage with local and national acts, a market, and food and drink stalls. Saturday 15 August, parade from 10:40am, event runs from 11am. Tickets from £5 early-bird / £7.50 advance (16+), £11 on the door; under-16s free.

Same weekend The Smoke & Fire Festival takes over Promenade Park, Maldon, for its 10th-anniversary edition — championship BBQ, live-fire cooking demos and music. Saturday 15 – Sunday 16 August. Day tickets from £19.80 adults, £8.40 children (5–16), family ticket (2 adults + 2 under-16s) £40.
Worth booking ahead for The Colchester Castle Summer Series opens in Castle Park on Thursday 20 August with Billy Ocean, the first of nine nights of big-name outdoor music running to 29 August. Just outside this week's window, but the kind of thing that sells out if you wait.
