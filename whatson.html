What's On

Colchester & Essex

Week of 9 November 2026

The week builds to Saturday at Firstsite: a free, all-day Art Fair with live demonstrations, workshops and festive family lantern-making, alongside a new exhibition pairing Jo Chapman and Molly Thomson that opens the same day. Further afield, the Essex County Christmas Fair opens its three-day run at Boreham House near Chelmsford from Friday. Garden open days stay out of season and dedicated kids' events are thin beyond Saturday.

⭐ Pick of the week

The Firstsite Art Fair, Saturday 14 November, 10am–5pm, the Foyer at Firstsite, free entry: a day-long fair of original paintings and sculpture, portfolio surgeries with artists Simon Carter, Justine Moss and Lisa Temple-Cox, an upcycling workshop, a talk on Jumbo (Colchester's water tower), festive family lantern-making, an origami workshop and live music from Panama City in the evening. Entry is free; some workshops are separately ticketed. Details at the Firstsite listing.

🌻 Open days

Gap this week Essex's National Garden Scheme season closed for the year in mid-October, and no Colchester-district garden or heritage open day could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listings directly for whatever opens next, likely not before spring.

👨‍👩‍👧 Kids & family

Festive Family Lantern Making — Saturday 14 November, 11am–4pm, Firstsite: a drop-in festive lantern workshop running through the day as part of the Art Fair (see Pick of the week). Details at the Firstsite listing.

Mini Movers & Makers Toddler Group — Thursday 19 November, 10am, Firstsite, ages 2–4 with a parent/carer, from £7.50: an hour of movement and making for toddlers. Details at the Firstsite listing.

🍴 Food and drink

Essex County Christmas Fair — Friday 13–Sunday 15 November, Boreham House, Chelmsford, ticketed, advance-booking discount ends 12 November: a three-day festive fair in the grounds with food and drink stalls, shopping marquees and appearances from Father Christmas. Tickets and details at the official booking page.
