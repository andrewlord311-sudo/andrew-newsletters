This week's headline is the Colchester Castle Summer Series arriving in Castle Park, with five big outdoor concerts running Thursday to Sunday and again next Thursday. Colchester Castle Summer Series — five nights of major outdoor concerts in Lower Castle Park: Billy Ocean (Thu 20 Aug, support Marti Pellow), The Hollywood Vampires — Alice Cooper, Joe Perry & Johnny Depp — (Fri 21 Aug, support The Damned), Becky Hill (Sat 22 Aug), Bastille (Sun 23 Aug, support Orla Gartland & Alfie Templeton), and Richard Ashcroft (Thu 27 Aug). Tickets — prices vary by night and tier; check the listing for the date you want.
