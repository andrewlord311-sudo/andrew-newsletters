Colchester & Essex

Week of 22 July 2026

The big one this week: Colchester Castle's Museum of the Moon opens Friday, kicking off a summer-long programme of moon trails and kids' clubs. A local theatre company also makes its Castle Park debut this week, and a 20-acre private garden throws open its gates for a summer fayre this weekend.

⭐ Pick of the week

Our Country's Good — local company Stage Craft makes its debut at Castle Park Theatre with Timberlake Wertenbaker's play, on the Lower Bowls Green in Castle Park. Runs Wed 22 – Sat 25 July; evening doors 6.30pm (show 7.30pm), plus a Saturday matinee (doors 2pm, show 3pm).

Worth booking ahead for Museum of the Moon opens at Colchester Castle on Friday 24 July and runs to 31 October — a six-metre lunar installation by artist Luke Jerram, lit and soundtracked, plus a self-guided Mystical Moon Trail. Part of the museums' summer programme running to 2 September.

🌻 Open days, gardens & food/drink

Summer Fayre at Green Island Gardens, Ardleigh — 20 acres of private garden open to the public for the weekend, with 30+ stalls (homemade produce, jewellery, crafts, woodwork, glasswork), a tearoom, licensed bar and live music. The garden's giant lilies are at their best right now. Sat 25 – Sun 26 July. £5/adult, free for children and season-ticket holders; wheelchair accessible; no dogs except assistance dogs. (The garden is also a regular National Garden Scheme opener, if you want to visit another time.)

Thin week for food & drink events Colchester's two big food festivals — the Sausage and Cider Fest and the main Food & Drink Festival — both already happened earlier this month. Beyond the Green Island Gardens fayre above, the standing option is Colchester Market on the High Street, running Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm.
