What's On

Colchester & Essex

Week of 7 September 2026

A quiet start gives way to a packed second weekend: Heritage Open Days begins across Essex on Friday, and Saturday 12 September alone brings Firstsite's free Festival of Rice and Spice, the two-day ALB Festival taking over Castle Park, and Colchester's Craft & Flea market the day after. Three current exhibitions — including a genuine dinosaur skeleton at Hollytrees — are also worth folding into a castle-quarter visit while they're on.

⭐ Pick of the week

Colchester's Festival of Rice and Spice, Saturday 12 September, 12pm–5pm at Firstsite: a free, long-running community celebration of the city's cultures through food stalls, music, dance and family workshops. Free entry. Details at the Visit Colchester listing.

Also worth diarising this week The same weekend carries two bigger draws. Heritage Open Days runs Fri 11 – Sun 20 Sep across Essex, free entry everywhere, with Colchester Castle, Hollytrees Museum, the Mercury Theatre and several churches all opening up — full programme at the Visit Colchester listing. And the ALB Festival — billed as Colchester's biggest live-music weekend, headlined by Scouting for Girls with Heather Small and S Club 7's Jon and Jo — runs Sat 12 & Sun 13 Sep at Lower Castle Park; some ticket tiers were showing as sold out at the time of writing, so check availability first at albfestival.com.
