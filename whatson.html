Colchester & Essex

Week of 27 July 2026

This week's headline act is a late one: Castle Park's after-dark theatre season brings The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes to the Lower Bowls Green for two nights running into the weekend, with an outdoor family Jungle Book and a gaming festival at Charter Hall filling the same Saturday. Further out, Beeleigh Abbey near Maldon opens its gardens for a single day and Colchester's monthly farmers' market returns to the Mercury Theatre piazza.

⭐ Pick of the week

The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes — Castle Park After Dark returns with a late-night production on the Lower Bowls Green in Castle Park, staged after the park has closed to the public, with the BARd area open for drinks and ice cream. Runs Fri 31 July – Sat 1 August; doors 8pm, show starts 9pm. £16 standard / £14 concessions.

Worth booking ahead for The Choir of Man plays the Mercury Theatre Tue 4 – Sat 8 August, with an extra midweek matinee added due to demand — a sign it's selling well. A pre-show afternoon tea is available ahead of the 4pm performance on Fri 7 August.

🌻 Open days, gardens & food/drink

Beeleigh Abbey Gardens Open Day, Maldon — the historic abbey gardens open for a single day, showcasing recent garden projects within the grounds. Admission is by pre-booked ticket only, with hourly arrival slots (stay as long as you like once in). Friday 7 August. Adult £8.50, child (5–16) £4.50, under 5s free.

On the food front, Colchester Farmers' Market returns to the Mercury Theatre piazza on Saturday 1 August, 9.30am–2pm, with over thirty stalls of local produce, bakes and street food. Beyond that, the standing option remains Colchester Market on the High Street, running Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm.
