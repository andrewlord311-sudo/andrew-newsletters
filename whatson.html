Colchester & Essex

Week of 3 August 2026

This week's headline is a genuinely unusual one: Colchester Town Hall — 800 years the seat of local justice — opens its cells, courtroom and council chambers for an after-dark ghost hunt on Friday, the same week Firstsite launches a fortnight of hot family lunches and the Mercury Theatre's Choir of Man plays its closing performances. Further out, big-name music returns to Castle Park for nine nights from 20 August.

⭐ Pick of the week

Colchester Town Hall Ghost Hunt — a rare chance inside the Moot Hall's underground tunnels, cells, courtroom and jury room after dark, investigating reports of a former town clerk said to still walk the halls. Saturday 8 August, doors from the evening; tea, coffee and biscuits included, torch recommended. Ages 18+.

Closing this week The Choir of Man plays its final performances at the Mercury Theatre up to and including Saturday 8 August — last chance to catch it before it moves on.
Worth booking ahead for The Colchester Castle Summer Series brings nine nights of big-name outdoor music to Castle Park, 20–29 August — Billy Ocean opens it, followed by Hollywood Vampires, Becky Hill, Bastille, Richard Ashcroft, JLS and Fatboy Slim across the run. Beyond the immediate week, but the kind of thing that sells out if you wait.
