What's On

Colchester & Essex

Week of 23 November 2026

A busier week than last: Firstsite gives over Saturday and Sunday to the Romanian Film Festival UK, and Thursday brings its regular Queer Film Social. Just outside Colchester, Hylands Estate's "Enlightened" light trail opens for the season on Friday. Garden open days remain the one gap — the National Garden Scheme's Essex season is still closed for winter.

⭐ Pick of the week

Romanian Film Festival UK at Firstsite: three special screenings over the weekend ahead of the festival's main London programme. On Our Own (Flavia, left largely to fend for herself while her parents work abroad, sees her fragile independence unravel when she learns they're divorcing) opens it on Saturday 28 November, 5pm, £11.50 standard / £10.50 concessions — tickets and details at the Firstsite listing. It's followed the same evening by 3 Days In September, 8pm, and on Sunday 29 November by Atlas Of The Universe, 2pm.

🌻 Open days

Gap this week The National Garden Scheme's Essex season remains closed for the winter, and no Colchester-district garden or heritage open day could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listings directly for whatever opens next, likely not before spring.

👨‍👩‍👧 Kids & family

Enlightened — Hylands Estate, Chelmsford, from Friday 27 November to 2 January 2027, timed entry 4.30pm–8pm, adult £12 / child (3–15) £8.50 / under-3s and carers free: the estate's winter light trail returns for its new season, around an hour's walk through illuminated grounds, with parking included. Tickets and timed slots at the Hylands Estate listing.

Family Air Dry Clay Winter Decorations — Sunday 29 November, 10am, Firstsite, suitable from age 4 with an accompanying adult, £11 standard / £8 Mosaic Member (each person making one needs their own ticket): an hour-long seasonal clay workshop. Details at the Firstsite listing.

Saturday Craft Time — Saturday 28 November, 10am–12pm, Greenstead Library, ages 4–8 (under-12s must be accompanied), free, no booking needed: a free monthly drop-in craft session. Details at the Essex Libraries listing.

🍴 Food and drink

Essex Makers Markets: Christmas at Harvester Rayleigh — Wednesday 25 November, 3pm–9pm, Harvester, Arterial Road, Rayleigh SS6 7SP, free entry: an evening of local makers' stalls alongside the pub's usual food and drink. Details at the VisitEssex listing.

No new Colchester-specific food or drink event could be confirmed for the coming week. The standing option remains Colchester's regular street market on Culver Street East & West and the High Street, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm — see the council's market page.
