Week of 5 October 2026

It's a comedy-and-spoken-word week rather than a festival one: Brian Bilston brings his deadpan poetry show to the Arts Centre on Saturday, with Nish Kumar and Jen Brister either side of him at Charter Hall. Gardens and food markets are both quiet for the coming week, so this issue leans on live events and the exhibitions already running at Firstsite and the Castle.

⭐ Pick of the week

Brian Bilston: How to Lay an Egg with a Horse Inside, Saturday 10 October, Colchester Arts Centre: the "unofficial Poet Laureate of Twitter" brings his deadpan, widely-loved comic verse to the Arts Centre's intimate former church setting. Details and booking at the Stereoboard listing.

Also worth diarising this week Two nights of stand-up at Charter Hall: Nish Kumar — Angry Humour from a Really Nice Guy, Thursday 8 October, doors 6:30pm, tickets from £24.50 (Stereoboard listing); and Jen Brister: Reactive, Saturday 10 October, 7:30pm, £27 plus booking fee, 18+ (Ents24 listing).

Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listing directly nearer the time in case one is added.
