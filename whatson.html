Colchester & Essex

Week of 28 September 2026

Lower Castle Park turns Bavarian this Friday and Saturday as the UK's touring Oktoberfest pitches its bierhall for the weekend. It's a big week for last chances too: Firstsite's Arpita Shah photography show closes Sunday, and a 0.9-acre country garden near Great Dunmow opens for just two afternoons.

⭐ Pick of the week

Oktoberfest Colchester, Friday 2 – Saturday 3 October, Lower Castle Park: a Bavarian beer bar, oompah bands, XL bier steins, bratwurst and pretzels, lederhosen and a mini trader village, billed as the UK's biggest touring Oktoberfest. 18+, tickets from £15. Details and booking at the Ents24 listing.

Also worth diarising this week Colchester Comedian of the Year Final 2026 — 18 emerging stand-ups whittled down over three heats compete for the Jo Fletcher-Cross Trophy, Tuesday 7 October, Three Wise Monkeys, doors 7pm, show 7:30–9:30pm. Details on the Three Wise Monkeys events calendar.
