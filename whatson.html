Colchester & Essex

Week of 24 August 2026

This is bank holiday finale week for the Colchester Castle Summer Series — Richard Ashcroft and JLS play Castle Park on Thursday and Friday, with Saturday's Fatboy Slim show already sold out. The last week of the school holidays also wraps up with Space Day at Colchester Castle and Firstsite's Holiday Fun Canteen, while the bank holiday weekend itself brings two open-garden events and two food festivals to the wider county.

⭐ Pick of the week

Colchester Castle Summer Series — finale weekend: Richard Ashcroft (Thu 27 Aug, gates 4pm, tickets around £70) and JLS with Gabrielle (Fri 28 Aug, gates 4pm, tickets around £55.45) close out the series in Lower Castle Park. Saturday's Fatboy Slim show (29 Aug, gates 2pm) is sold out. See the eFestivals listing for ticket links and full details.

Worth booking ahead for Just after this window, Extreme Robots — Europe's biggest touring robot-combat show, with Robot Wars and BattleBots veterans — comes to Charter Hall, Colchester Leisure World on Sat 5 Sep, with shows at 12pm and 4pm. Tickets from £23.65 at Eventim.

🌻 Open days, gardens & food/drink

Rayleigh Garden Trail — 21 private gardens open over the bank holiday weekend (Sat 29 – Mon 31 Aug, 10am–5pm), with lunches and refreshments at Rayleigh Parish Centre. £5 admission covers the whole weekend, in aid of Friends of Holy Trinity, Rayleigh. Trail details.

Ulting Wick — this National Garden Scheme garden near Maldon opens Mon 31 Aug, 2–5pm, no pre-booking; cash only on the gate. NGS listing.

Brightlingsea Food & Drink Festival — street food, arts, crafts and local produce on the seafront, Sat 26 Aug (11am–7pm) and Sun 27 Aug (11am–5pm). Adults £3, children free. Festival details.

Maldon Food & Drink Festival — street food from around the world, craft beer, cider and gin bars and artisan produce at Promenade Park, Sat 29 – Sun 30 Aug, 11am–6pm. Tickets.

For a standing option any other day this week, Colchester Market runs on the High Street Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm.
