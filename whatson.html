Colchester & Essex

Week of 16 November 2026

A quieter week: the one stand-out date is a captioned National Theatre Live screening of Golden Boy, starring Josh O'Connor, at Firstsite on Friday. Beyond that it's mostly steady ground — Firstsite's three current exhibitions continue, Hollytrees' dinosaur show keeps drawing families, and there's no new garden open day or food/drink event to report this week.

⭐ Pick of the week

National Theatre Live: Golden Boy (Captioned), Friday 20 November, 6pm, Firstsite, £20.50 standard / £18.50 concessions: a captioned screening of the Almeida Theatre's filmed revival of Clifford Odets' Depression-era classic, starring Josh O'Connor as a talented young violinist drawn into professional boxing — directed by Sam Yates, filmed live with a string quartet, running 195 minutes. Tickets at the Firstsite listing.

🌻 Open days

Gap this week Essex's National Garden Scheme season remains closed for the winter, and no Colchester-district garden or heritage open day could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listings directly for whatever opens next, likely not before spring.

👨‍👩‍👧 Kids & family

Discover: Museum Wonders — Hollytrees Museum, Monday–Saturday, timed entry, to 30 November 2027, adults £5 / children (4–16) £3.50 / family saver (up to 2 adults + 3 children) £15: a six-metre dinosaur skeleton, Juliasaurus, on public display for the first time, alongside local fossils and oddities including witch bottles and shoes hidden under floorboards for luck. Details and timed slots at the Colchester Museums listing.

Mini Movers & Makers Toddler Group — Thursday 19 November, 10am, Firstsite, ages 2–4 with a parent/carer, £7.50 first child / £4 each additional child: an hour of artist-led movement, sensory storytelling and making for toddlers. Details at the Firstsite listing.

Tenzing (Parent & Baby screening) — Thursday 19 November, 11am, Firstsite, £9 standard / £8 concessions / £4 students and under-18s: a relaxed, buggy-friendly screening of the true story of Tenzing Norgay's ascent of Everest alongside Edmund Hillary. Details at the Firstsite listing.

🍴 Food and drink

Gap this week No new food or drink event could be confirmed for the coming week. The standing option remains Colchester's regular street market on Culver Street East & West and the High Street, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm. Further ahead, the council has confirmed its Annual Christmas Market for Sunday 6 December, High Street, 10am–5pm, with up to 80 stalls and free entry — see the council's market page.
