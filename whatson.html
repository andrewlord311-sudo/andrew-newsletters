Colchester & Essex

Week of 2 November 2026

A quieter week now half term is over: the headline is a last chance to see Firstsite's Duncan Grant exhibition before it closes on Sunday. Firstsite's monthly Folk & Bespoke artisan market returns to the foyer on Saturday, and Hylands House near Chelmsford opens its grounds, stables studios and a street food market for its monthly Open Estate Day on Sunday. Garden open days and dedicated kids' programming both stay thin this week.

⭐ Pick of the week

Duncan Grant: Worlds Through Desire, last chance — closes Sunday 8 November, Firstsite, free: drawings and paintings exploring LGBT+ friendship, love and intimacy, in its final week before coming down. Details at the Art Fund listing.

🌻 Open days

Also worth diarising this week Hylands Open Estate Day — Sunday 8 November, 10am–3pm, Hylands House, Chelmsford: the house and grounds open to explore at your own pace, with a library exhibition and guided tours on the history of Hylands, the Stables artist studios open to browse, a street food market with handmade gifts and baked goods, and live music from Hot Box Live in the courtyard (Hylands Estate listing).
Gap this week Essex's National Garden Scheme season closed for the year in mid-October, and no Colchester-district garden open day could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listings directly for whatever opens next, likely not before spring.

👨‍👩‍👧 Kids & family

Gap this week With half term over, dedicated kids' programming is thin for the coming week. The one confirmed family session is Firstsite's Mini Movers & Makers Toddler Group (ages 2–4 with a parent/carer), Thursday 5 November, 10am, Firstsite — details and cost at the Firstsite listing. Nothing else aimed specifically at kids could be confirmed with a dated 2026 source this week.

🍴 Food and drink

Folk & Bespoke Artisan Craft Market — Saturday 7 November, 10am–3pm, the Foyer at Firstsite, free, no booking required: the monthly market of local artisan craft and food stalls returns. Details at the Firstsite listing.
