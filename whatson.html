Colchester & Essex

Week of 26 October 2026

Half term is in full swing: Colchester Zoo's Shriek Week runs all week with an evening Trail of Terror on three nights, and Firstsite has a run of half-term drop-ins topped off with a Friday-evening willow-lantern workshop. The Castle's Museum of the Moon enters its very last week before coming down on Saturday, and Firstsite opens a new free photography exhibition — but garden open days and new food events stay quiet.

⭐ Pick of the week

Make Your Own Willow Lantern, Friday 30 October, 5:30–9:30pm, Firstsite: a half-term evening workshop weaving your own willow lantern to take home. £21 standard, £16 for Mosaic members, at the Firstsite listing.

Also worth diarising this week Museum of the Moon — Luke Jerram's six-metre lunar installation — is in its final week at Colchester Castle, closing Saturday 31 October, 10am–5pm daily, with a small surcharge on top of Castle admission (Visit Essex listing).

🌻 Open days

Gap this week Essex's National Garden Scheme season ran to 16 October and has now closed for the year, and no open day for Colchester or the wider district could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listings directly for whatever opens next, likely not before spring.
