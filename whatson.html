What's On

Colchester & Essex

Week of 21 September 2026

Firstsite unveils its biggest show in years this week: Simon Carter's From the Landscape turns the whole gallery into a walk-through Essex-coast painting. Saturday and Sunday bring a 500-drone Alice in Wonderland spectacular to Castle Park, and a Georgian farmhouse garden near Saffron Walden opens its gates for one afternoon only.

⭐ Pick of the week

Simon Carter: From the Landscape, opened Saturday 19 September, Firstsite: the most ambitious exhibition of the Essex artist's 40-year career, with paintings spilling directly onto the gallery walls, a 19-metre wall of 500 monochrome sketches, and a soundscape built from recordings of the Essex coast. Free, no booking. Runs to 17 January 2027. Details at the Firstsite listing.

Also worth diarising this week A Curious Adventure: The Alice in Wonderland Drone Spectacular — over 500 drones, lasers and lighting bring the story to life above Castle Park, Saturday 25 & Sunday 26 September, gates 6pm, show at 7:45pm, with street food stalls after. Ticketed, entrance via Cowdray Crescent CO1 1TJ. Details and booking at the official Alice Drone Show site.

🍴 Food and drink

Cask Beer Week runs to Sunday 27 September, 5pm–10pm at Three Wise Monkeys, celebrating Other Monkey Brewing's cask ales, rolling into an Oktoberfest Bierhall weekend, 24–27 September (Friday–Sunday sittings, booking required — call 01206 543014). Details on the Three Wise Monkeys events calendar.

Gap this week Colchester Farmers' Market falls on the first Saturday of the month, so it's not back until 3 October.

For a standing option any day this week, Colchester Market runs on the High Street Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm.
