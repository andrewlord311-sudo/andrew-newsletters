Colchester & Essex

Week of 1 September 2026

A quieter start to the week — Mark Chadwick of the Levellers plays an acoustic set at Colchester Arts Centre on Wednesday — before Saturday 5 September brings everything at once: the free citywide charity spectacular Invasion Colchester, a heavyweight robot-combat show at Charter Hall, and the monthly farmers' market on the Piazza. Ulting Wick's late-summer garden has one more open afternoon on Friday. And the weekend after is already worth diarising, with Heritage Open Days and Firstsite's Festival of Rice and Spice both landing on Saturday 12 September.

⭐ Pick of the week

Invasion Colchester: the city centre's annual charity takeover returns Saturday 5 September, from 10am, with costumed heroes from Star Wars, Marvel and beyond around Culver Square and the High Street, and Saber Guild UK bringing lightsaber training to the Dragonfly Lounge for young Padawans, plus a costume competition across four age categories. Free entry, in aid of St Helena Hospice, Colchester & Ipswich Hospitals Charity, and Mind (Mid & North East Essex). Full schedule at the Invasion Colchester website.

Worth booking ahead for Two things land the following weekend. Heritage Open Days runs Fri 11 – Sun 20 Sep across Essex, free entry everywhere; Colchester Castle opens up on Sat 12 Sep (10am–4.30pm) with chariot and ballista demonstrations — full programme at the Visit Colchester listing. The same Saturday, Firstsite hosts its Festival of Rice and Spice from midday, a free celebration of the city's communities through food, music and performance — details at the Firstsite event page.

🌻 Open days, gardens & food/drink

Ulting Wick — this National Garden Scheme garden near Maldon has one more late-summer opening this window: Fri 4 Sep, 2–5pm. No pre-booking; £7.50 adults, children free; cash only on the gate (poor mobile signal for cards). NGS listing.

No new food festival turned up for this window — the standing option is Colchester Farmers' Market, back on the Piazza outside the Mercury Theatre Sat 5 Sep, 9.30am–2pm, free entry, with around thirty stalls of local produce. Market details. For grown-ups fancying something different, Firstsite's Boozy Brushes — a drag-hosted portrait painting class with a drink included — is running various dates through 25 Sep, 18+. Details and dates. For a standing option any other day this week, Colchester Market runs on the High Street Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 9am–4pm.
