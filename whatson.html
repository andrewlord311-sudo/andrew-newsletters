Colchester & Essex

Week of 12 October 2026

A quieter week than last weekend's run of comedy: the one unmissable date is a genuine prog-rock legend at the Arts Centre, half term and the zoo's Halloween trail are starting to show up on the calendar for the back end of this window, and the Castle's six-metre moon has its final fortnight before coming down. Gardens and food markets stay thin for the coming week.

⭐ Pick of the week

Wishbone Ash, Tuesday 13 October, 7:30pm, Colchester Arts Centre: the pioneering twin-lead-guitar rock band — still touring more than five decades after "Argus" — play the Arts Centre's intimate former-church room. Tickets from £37.90 inc. fees at the Stereoboard listing.

Also worth diarising this week Luke Combs UK — a touring tribute act, not the American star himself — brings a live five-piece band's set of his songs to Charter Hall, Thursday 15 October, 7:30–10pm, guide price £29.50 (Visit Essex listing).

🌻 Open days

Gap this week Essex's National Garden Scheme season is winding down for the year and no specific garden open day for Colchester or the wider district could be confirmed for the coming week. Worth checking the National Garden Scheme's Essex listing directly nearer the time in case one is added.
