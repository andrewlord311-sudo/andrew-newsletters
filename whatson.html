What's On

Colchester & Essex

Week of 14 September 2026

Heritage Open Days reaches its final week, with free walks, an organ recital and an archaeology tour opening up buildings that are normally closed to the public. Saturday brings the free Family Fun Festival back to Castle Park for bouncy castles and a dog show, and four current exhibitions across Firstsite and the castle quarter — including a genuine dinosaur skeleton at Hollytrees — are still on for anyone folding in a museum visit.

⭐ Pick of the week

Colchester Free Family Fun Festival, Saturday 19 September, 11am–4pm at Castle Park: bouncy castles, face painting, games, live music, a BBQ, a Tiny Tea Tent and a dog show. Free entry. Details at the Visit Essex listing.

Also worth diarising this week Heritage Open Days runs to Sunday 20 September, free entry across Essex. Two highlights: John Constable's Colchester, a guided walk retracing the places the painter knew and sketched — Tuesday 15 September, Mercury Theatre, pre-booking required (details at the Heritage Open Days listing) — and Meet and Play the Moot Hall Organ at Colchester Town Hall, a rare chance to hear and play the restored 1902 organ — Saturday 19 September, no booking needed (details at the Heritage Open Days listing).
