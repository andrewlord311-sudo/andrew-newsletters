Gentle Logic Masters Deutschland

Sudoku Variants Series (045) — Quadruple Sudoku

from the Rätselportal's long-running Sudoku Variants Series
Normal sudoku rules apply: digits 1–9 in every row, column and 3×3 box. Additionally, each set of four digits printed at the intersection of four cells tells you which digits appear in those four surrounding cells. A clean single-constraint variant — the whole series exists to introduce one variant at a time properly, so it teaches the technique rather than assuming you already have it.

Gentle Logic Masters Deutschland

Sudoku Variants Series (048) — XV Sudoku

from the Rätselportal's Sudoku Variants Series
Normal sudoku rules, plus Roman numerals between neighbouring cells: an X means those two digits add to 10, a V means they add to 5. The catch that makes XV worth doing properly is what the absence of a marker tells you — in most XV puzzles every such pair is marked, so a blank edge actively rules out those sums.

Gentle New daily source

Genuinely Approachable Sudoku (GAS) — a new variant every day

by the GAS team (Clover, Philip, Bill and guests)
A daily variant sudoku with a deliberate design brief: the constraint changes every day — killer, arrow, thermo, XV, whispers and so on — but each puzzle is built to be solvable rather than brutal, and each comes with a target time so you can benchmark yourself. Started by Clover in June 2021 and now published daily on Grandmaster Puzzles as well. The monthly "GAS Leak" PDF compilations collect every puzzle from a given month with SudokuPad and f-puzzles play links.

🌍 The wider puzzle worldThree wells you almost certainly aren't drawing from already

Medium Nikoli, Japan

Slitherlink, from the people who invented it

Nikoli — the publisher that gave sudoku its modern form
Connect the dots with a single continuous loop that never crosses or branches. Each number tells you exactly how many of the four edges around it the loop uses — and the only numbers in play are 0, 1, 2 and 3. Nikoli's own framing is the appealing bit: the depth comes from an effectively unlimited stock of reusable local patterns solvers call "theorems", which you accumulate rather than compute. Their English site has the rules and sample puzzles for dozens of genres you won't have met — Heyawake, Masyu, Shikaku, Hashiwokakero.

Tough Grandmaster Puzzles

The Grandmaster Puzzles pencil-puzzle archives

Thomas Snyder's "The Art of Puzzles" and its guest setters
The same "extra constraint layer" DNA as variant sudoku, but on genres that have nothing to do with placing 1–9. The archives are sorted by genre and hand-graded for difficulty, so you can pick a lane and work down it: Fillomino (divide the grid into regions where every region of size n contains the number n, and no two same-size regions touch), Nurikabe (shade a single connected "wall" that isolates each numbered island at exactly its stated size), Kakuro and Cave. Championship-tier construction — these are the puzzles world-championship setters write for each other.

hosted by Logic Masters India, 11–18 October 2026

➗ Maths puzzle of the weekFrom Jane Street's monthly puzzle — a different lineage to the usual listicle sources

"Andy's Afternoon Amble" — Jane Street, August 2026
Andy the ant lives on a truncated tetrahedron blown up into a sphere: four white hexagons and four black triangles. He avoids the black triangles, suspecting they may be bottomless pits. Each afternoon he wakes on a white hexagon, marks it with pheromones as home, and ambles at random — each step going to one of the three neighbouring white hexagons with equal probability. He stops the moment he first returns home. How long is his amble, on average?
Their own worked example to get you started: on exactly 1/3 of afternoons the amble is 2 steps long — he visits one of the three neighbours, then has a 1/3 chance of stepping straight back home. The rest is where it gets interesting. (This is the sequel to their 2022 "Andy's Morning Stroll", if you want a warm-up.)
